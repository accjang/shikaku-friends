## description
Split the board into rectangles — one number in each, and the number tells its size!

Shikaku Friends brings the classic Japanese logic puzzle "Shikaku" to life with adorable animal friends.

■ Two simple rules
· Divide the board into rectangles.
· Each rectangle holds exactly one number, equal to its number of cells.

■ Features
· Drag to draw, tap to erase
· Kitty, Panda, Bunny, Shiba and Duckling move, cheer you on — pick your buddy
· 200 stages, and every puzzle has exactly one solution
· Collect decorations as you clear stages and build your friends' village
· Finish a board to reveal a hidden picture
· A new Daily Puzzle every day with 7-day streak rewards — share your result
· No timers to stress you out, plays offline
